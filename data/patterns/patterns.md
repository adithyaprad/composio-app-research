# Patterns across 100 apps (`data/runs/pass3`)

Generated by `scripts/patterns.py` from `rows.jsonl`, `needs_human.jsonl` and the gold/holdout score files. Every number below is computed from those inputs; examples are picked from each pattern's matching rows (high confidence first, then id).

## Headline patterns

### 1. Buildable today vs gated

**84 of 100 apps (84.0%) are buildable now without vendor approval. The other 16 need vendor approval: 8 gated by sales and 8 by a partner program, and 100.0% of gated apps land on buildable_with_approval.**

- Count: 84 / 100 (84.0%)
- Examples: Salesforce (`salesforce`), Pylon (`pylon`), Meta Ads (`meta-ads`)
- Reliability: **moderate** (fields: access, verdict)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: verdict scored gold 85%.

### 2. Official MCP adoption and Composio's uncovered opportunity

**86 of 100 apps (86.0%) ship an official MCP server. Of the 22 apps with no Composio toolkit, 15 are buildable now, 15 have an official MCP, and 11 are both.**

- Count: 11 / 22 (50.0%)
- Examples: Zoho CRM (`zoho-crm`), LiveAgent (`liveagent`), Vonage (`vonage`)
- Reliability: **moderate** (fields: mcp_status, verdict, composio_toolkit)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: mcp_status scored holdout 80%; verdict scored gold 85%.

### 3. Category and coverage unevenness

**developer_infra_and_data is the strongest category (100.0% buildable now, 100.0% official MCP, 0.0% needs_human); ecommerce is the hardest (6 of 10 buildable now, 30.0% Composio coverage). Lowest Composio coverage: ecommerce; most needs_human: ecommerce, support_and_helpdesk. Composio covers 82.1% of buildable-now apps vs 56.2% of the rest.**

- Count: 6 / 10 (60.0%)
- Examples: Squarespace (`squarespace`), Salesforce Commerce Cloud (`salesforce-commerce-cloud`), Magento (Adobe Commerce) (`magento-adobe-commerce`)
- Reliability: **moderate** (fields: verdict, mcp_status, composio_toolkit)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: verdict scored gold 85%; mcp_status scored holdout 80%.

### 4. Auth landscape

**66 of 100 apps (66.0%) use OAuth2 as primary auth. 48 offer both OAuth2 and API keys, 15 are OAuth2-only and 30 are API-key-only.**

- Count: 66 / 100 (66.0%)
- Examples: HubSpot (`hubspot`), Pipedrive (`pipedrive`), Attio (`attio`)
- Reliability: **caveated** (fields: auth_methods / primary_auth)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: auth_methods / primary_auth scored holdout 60%.

### 5. Human-in-the-loop necessity

**36 of 100 rows (36.0%) are flagged needs_human; top reasons: confidence_not_high (24), sales_or_partner_gate (16), conflicting_or_caveated_docs (15). The rate is 59.1% on apps with no Composio toolkit vs 29.5% on covered apps.**

- Count: 36 / 100 (36.0%)
- Examples: Zendesk (`zendesk`), Pylon (`pylon`), LiveAgent (`liveagent`)
- Reliability: **moderate** (fields: access, verdict)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: verdict scored gold 85%.

### 6. End-user admin and API breadth friction

**19 of 100 apps (19.0%) are marked as needing a customer admin to approve install (6 of 16 gated apps), and 81 have a broad API. These fields scored below the caveat threshold on validation, so read them as rough signals.**

- Count: 19 / 100 (19.0%)
- Examples: Salesforce (`salesforce`), HubSpot (`hubspot`), Attio (`attio`)
- Reliability: **caveated** (fields: requires_end_user_admin, api_breadth)
- Caveat: Lower-accuracy fields: requires_end_user_admin scored gold 70% / holdout 10%; api_breadth scored gold 65%.
